## Hackathon Program

The Laser Interferometer Space Antenna (LISA) is one of the European Space Agency flagship mission and will be the first space-based gravitational wave observatory, opening a new observational window in the millihertz frequency regime. To fully exploit LISA's scientific potential, we must extract gravitational wave signals from a complex mixture of noise and overlapping sources. This hackathon aims to develop machine learning approaches that combine the speed of neural networks with the statistical rigor of Bayesian inference to address LISA data analysis challenges.

## Travelling Information

ESTEC is located in Noordwijk, on the Dutch coast, approximately 20–30 minutes from Leiden and 30–40 minutes from Amsterdam Schiphol Airport.

### Overall Useful Links and Tips

- [9292](https://9292.nl/en) is the best tool for door-to-door travel planning in the Netherlands. Install the app before arrival.  
- The [NS (Dutch Railways)](https://www.ns.nl/en) app is useful for train schedules and tickets.  
- Uber is available but less reliable than in other countries—do not rely on it.  
- Renting a bike is often the most efficient way to move locally (especially from nearby accommodation in Noordwijk or Leiden).
